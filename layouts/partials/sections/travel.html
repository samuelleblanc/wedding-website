Getting here & staying nearby

Travel & Stay

✦

✈️ By Air

The closest airports are San Jose (SJC) — 45 min away — and San Francisco (SFO), about 75 min. Both offer rental cars and rideshare access.

🚗 By Car

From San Jose: Take Hwy 17 south to the Felton/Mt. Hermon exit, then follow signs to Roaring Camp. Parking at the venue is free.

🏨 Where to Stay

We recommend staying in Santa Cruz (15 min) or Scotts Valley (10 min). A room block will be available soon — check back here for the link.

🚕 Getting Around

We encourage rideshare (Uber/Lyft) so everyone can celebrate freely. The venue is located at the end of Graham Hill Rd — allow extra time for GPS quirks in the mountains.
