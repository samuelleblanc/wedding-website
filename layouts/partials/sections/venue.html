Roaring Camp Railroads

✦

Nestled in the heart of the Santa Cruz Mountains, Roaring Camp is one of Northern California's most magical settings. Ancient coast redwoods — some over 1,800 years old — stand as silent witnesses to the day we say "I do."

The venue sits along Bear Creek, surrounded by old-growth redwood groves, the scent of pine, and the gentle sound of a narrow-gauge railroad winding through the forest.
